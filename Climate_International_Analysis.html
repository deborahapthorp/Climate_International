International Climate Project Data Analysis

Author

Deborah Apthorp

Introduction

This project combines survey data from young people aged 16 - 26 from Chile, Hungary, and two different time points in Australia (2020 and 2022). The surveys covered demographics (age, gender, employment status, family status, number of people living in the household, number of children, subjective financial situation compared to the national average, family affluence scale, and type of municipality lived in ), and a number of questionnaires - not completely even across samples, but including the Climate Change Anxiety Scale (CCAS), the Depression, Anxiety and Stress Scale (DASS-21), the Psychological Distance Scale (PDS), the Hogg Eco-Anxiety Scale (HEAS-13), and the Brief Resilience Scale (BRS). There is also the Nature Relatedness Scale (NR-6) and the 5-item WHO Well-being Questionnaire for some of the samples only. There were also some open-ended questions and some about direct experiences of climate change events.

Brief commentary on the descriptives

The Municipalities data for Chile needs cleaning up.

From the descriptives, we can see that the Chilean sample is younger, on average, and has more male participants.

It looks as if there are differences between countries on the DASS and CCAS - we will test that later.

The crossover between countries with which scales have been collected is quite variable.
